Data & methodology

This explorer uses school-level public data from Tennessee, NCES, and the U.S. Census Bureau. Each metric retains its source and reporting year. The application does not create an overall school ranking.

The current bundle includes 2023–24 NCES campus points, 2024 Census ZCTA search centers, and the most recent downloaded 2024–25 TDOE metrics. Reporting years can differ by indicator.

Sources

  • Tennessee Department of Education data downloads: school accountability assessment/proficiency, TVAAS composite, and official A–F letter grades.
  • NCES EDGE public school locations: campus address, NCES identifier, and geographic point.
  • U.S. Census 2024 ZCTA Gazetteer: ZIP-area search center. ZIP codes and ZCTAs are not identical.

How schools are joined

The preferred application identifier is TN-{LEA}-{school ID} when the official TDOE identifiers are available. NCES campus records are joined to school reporting records using an exact school name normalized for case and punctuation, and only when the candidate is unique. TDOE LEA 190 is cross-referenced to NCES LEA 4703180. Conflicting or ambiguous name joins are left unmatched and reported in data/quality_report.json.

What the measures mean

  • ELA and Math proficiency are the TDOE published percentage of all students meeting or exceeding the applicable standard in the school accountability assessment file. Suppressed values remain unavailable.
  • TVAAS is Tennessee's schoolwide academic growth level on the 1–5 scale. Growth is distinct from proficiency.
  • School letter grade is the official Tennessee A–F accountability grade for its reporting year.

Geography and distance

Campus points come from NCES EDGE. Search origins use Census ZCTA internal points as approximate ZIP search centers. The ZIP field accepts Tennessee 37xxx codes represented in the cached Gazetteer; its suggestions remain limited to the 24 curated Nashville-area codes. Distances use a Haversine great-circle calculation with a 3,958.8 mile Earth radius. Results only include campuses with validated coordinates.

Gaps and responsible use

Enrollment and grade spans are sourced from the 2024-25 TDOE School Profile; graduation rates are not yet integrated. School governance type, charter/magnet status, zoning, historical trends, and school-specific report-card links are not yet enriched. The app is informational; it does not determine assignment, guarantee admission, or replace the official Tennessee State Report Card.
